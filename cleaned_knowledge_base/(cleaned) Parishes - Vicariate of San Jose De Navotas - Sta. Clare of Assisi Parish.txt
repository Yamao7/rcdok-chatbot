Our Heritage
The parish is devoted to Saint Clare, a devout follower of Saint Francis of Assisi, renowned for her devotion to prayer and poverty. It provides regular liturgical services, like as catechetical programs, novenas, and daily masses. Inspired by Saint Clare, the church’s architecture is simple yet inviting. The parish actively promotes faith and community involvement through programs for women, families, and young people. Additionally, social outreach initiatives are carried out to assist the community’s less fortunate people. Unusual festivities, such as Sta. Clare is characterized by lively religious and cultural activities. The parish places a strong emphasis on service to others, humility, and spiritual development.
